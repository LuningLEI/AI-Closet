For each assessment return four criteria with verdict pass/fail/uncertain and a concrete item-specific reason:

3. color_pattern: busy/statement top plus busy/statement bottom fails; a pattern with plain or two plain garments passes when visually coherent. Accept coordinated monochrome/tonal sports sets, black-white, light green-brown and blue-yellow solid contrasts. Texture such as denim, mesh or knit is not inherently a print. Color difference alone is never a failure.
4. occasion: garment purposes/formality work together. White shirts and black pencil skirts are good office candidates. WHITE SHIRT + BLUE DENIM SHORTS is a valid casual combination, not a failure merely because shirt metadata says Work. Pink blouses are not inherently informal. Avoid clearly incompatible specialized ski/gym and professional garments. Different occasion labels or formality numbers alone are insufficient to fail.
